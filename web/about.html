The Right Trousers

Every touch. Every point. Your team.

The Right Trousers is an ultimate frisbee scorekeeping and statistics app for browsers, iPad, and Android tablets. Record points, passes, turnovers, goals, lines, and defensive assignments, then review player details and linked game videos.

A shared team library

Authorized teammates can work from the same game library across devices. Players can view their teammates’ Player Details pages. Stats users record and correct games. Leadership can review team-wide player statistics and manage their assigned library.

Why sign in with Google?

Google sign-in identifies your account so the app can apply the team permissions assigned by an administrator. It requests your basic identity information: name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or Google Calendar.

Signing in alone does not grant access to team data. Contact your team’s administrator for access.
