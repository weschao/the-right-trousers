The Right Trousers

Privacy

Updated November 16, 2026

The Right Trousers records ultimate frisbee games and provides a shared library for authorized team members. This page describes the information used by the app.

Google sign-in information

Google sign-in provides basic account information, including your name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier, to Supabase Authentication. The app uses your authenticated account identifier and email address to identify your account and apply its assigned permissions. The app does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or Google Calendar.

Team and game information

The shared library stores submitted teams, rosters, player details, groups, games, play-by-play events, notes, settings, and video links in Supabase. Submitted video setup information may also be stored with a game to support video review. Changes are versioned, and the backend retains an audit history and records of deleted items.

Access depends on roles assigned by an administrator. Players receive authorized Player Details pages; stats users can read and edit authorized games; leadership can access the library within their assigned scope. Global administrators can access all libraries. Account creation by itself does not grant team access.

Information on your device

Your browser stores authentication session information and pending sign-in state so you can sign in and use the app. Local libraries from earlier device-only use may remain in IndexedDB. JSON backups downloaded by a user remain wherever that user saves them. Shared mode requires an internet connection to save edits.

Service providers and videos

Google handles Google account sign-in, Supabase handles authentication and shared data storage, and Cloudflare hosts the production website. These services process information needed to provide those functions. Opening or loading a linked video may connect your device to its video provider, such as YouTube or Veo, whose privacy practices also apply.

Questions and data requests

Contact weschao@gmail.com about account access, corrections, exports, or removal of stored account or team information. Team administrators also manage memberships and library records. You can review Google sign-in grants in your Google Account settings.
